A Letter from Our Representative

Dear, People of Tomorrow!

Since it was officially established in 2012, the Asian Medical Students' Association Universitas Muhammadiyah Palembang, known as AMSA-UMP, has produced many alumni who excel in a wide range of fields.

AMSA-UMP is committed to giving its members superb and meaningful experiences. Guided by AMSA's core philosophies, every program is designed to create impact.

  • Knowledge
  • Action
  • Friendship

Through this official website, we present our organization. We hope it gives you a brief introduction to our values and activities.

“Unifying Visionaries, Redefining Boundaries”

Viva AMSA!
